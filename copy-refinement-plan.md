# Calden Terre Website Copy Refinement Plan

Prepared against the live site (`C-Terre/calden-terre-website`, Next.js, static export). This is a positioning and copy refinement. No design, layout, navigation, color, typography, animation, or component changes are proposed. Every change below is a text edit, plus two purely structural additions the new positioning requires: a "Who We Serve" section on the homepage and two new fields on the contact form. Both additions reuse existing CSS patterns (the same asymmetric two-column grid already used for the contact section, and the same field styling already used for name and email) rather than introducing new visual language.

Sections not listed as changed below can be treated as remaining substantially unchanged.

---

## 1. Hero (Home)

**Current positioning problem:** The h1 ("Sustainability solutions that move beyond strategy") and lead paragraph name "SMEs and Community Development Financial Institutions" as one equal audience, with no implementation-gap framing and no mention of established, operating businesses specifically.

**Recommended change:** Replace the lead paragraph with the exact new positioning statement. Sharpen the h1 to name implementation and the audience without overloading it. Swap the secondary CTA from "View Our Work" to "Start a Project Conversation" as primary, keeping "Explore Our Services" as secondary, per the required CTA pair.

**Proposed replacement copy:**
- H1: "Sustainability implementation for growing businesses."
- Lead: "Calden Terre is a sustainability implementation consultancy helping established small and growing businesses turn sustainability goals into practical systems, measurable actions and credible progress."
- Small eyebrow line: "Sustainability • Implementation • Credible Progress"
- Primary CTA: "Start a Project Conversation" → /contact
- Secondary CTA: "Explore Our Services" → /services

**Reason:** Leads with the exact required positioning statement, names the primary audience immediately, and drops the "SMEs and CDFIs as equal audience" framing without touching layout.

---

## 2. What We Do (Home services teaser)

**Current positioning problem:** Six cards read as separate services rather than one connected implementation system, and card three ("Climate Impact & Infrastructure Innovation") drifts from the ten capability areas in the new positioning.

**Recommended change:** Reword the section heading to state explicitly that the capabilities work together. Re-map the six cards onto the ten required capability areas (readiness and strategy, implementation and execution, operational systems, measurement and data, climate and responsible sourcing, reporting and continuous improvement), folding program and project management into implementation rather than giving it a separate card.

**Proposed replacement copy:**
- H2: "Connected capabilities that turn ambition into practical implementation"
- Cards: Readiness and Strategy / Implementation and Execution / Operational Systems and Process Improvement / Measurement and Decision Support / Climate Impact and Responsible Sourcing / Reporting and Continuous Improvement (full text below in the complete copy)

**Reason:** Directly answers the instruction that capabilities should read as connected elements of implementation, not a list of unrelated services, and removes a card that no longer maps to the current specialization.

---

## 3. Calden Terre Impact Framework (Home teaser and Framework page)

**Current positioning problem:** Stage summaries mention "ESG & climate performance" and describe stages generically, without tying them to the implementation gap or the new audience.

**Recommended change:** Keep the framework name and six-stage sequence exactly as is (Discover, Strategize, Implement, Measure, Optimize, Sustain). Refine each stage's description so the sequence reads as one path from understanding an implementation gap to embedding continuous improvement.

**Proposed replacement copy:** See complete copy section below for the homepage teaser and the full Framework page. Descriptions follow the exact substance requested: Discover understands the business and its gaps, Strategize sets practical priorities and an achievable roadmap, Implement puts agreed systems into operation, Measure establishes KPIs and monitors performance, Optimize uses evidence to improve, Sustain embeds accountability and continuous improvement.

**Reason:** Preserves the framework exactly as instructed while making its purpose legible against the new specialization.

---

## 4. Who We Serve (new section, Home)

**Current positioning problem:** No section currently states audience structure at all. The site implies SMEs and CDFIs as one audience through the hero and footer language alone.

**Recommended change:** Add a new homepage section, placed after the Framework section and before Selected Work, with established small and growing businesses presented first and larger, and a clearly secondary card for institutional and business-support partners.

**Proposed replacement copy:**
- Eyebrow: "Who We Serve"
- H2: "Built primarily for established small and growing businesses"
- Primary card: "Established Small and Growing Businesses," full text below
- Secondary card: "Institutional and Business-Support Partners," full text below, includes a "Discuss a Sponsored Business Program" link

**Reason:** This is the section the new positioning most directly requires and the one place a visitor gets an explicit, ordered statement of who the primary client is and how institutional partners fit in, without implying CDFIs are an equal target market.

---

## 5. Selected Work (Home teaser and Selected Work page)

**Current positioning problem:** None specific to the repositioning. No SME/CDFI language appears here, and the work items are real, specific, and already implementation-focused.

**Recommended change:** Leave the Selected Work page and its four case studies unchanged, since altering factual work history is out of scope and not requested. Minor language tightening only on the homepage teaser heading for consistency of voice.

**Reason:** Per the brief, do not fabricate or alter results, clients, or metrics. This section already demonstrates assessment, strategy translation, implementation, measurement, and continuous improvement, exactly what the new positioning wants proven.

---

## 6. Resource Spotlight (Home) and Resources page

**Current positioning problem:** The Assessment description names "SMEs and CDFIs" as one equal audience, and neither the homepage spotlight nor the Resources page states the Assessment-to-Codex-to-Advisory journey, or the parallel institutional pathway.

**Recommended change:** Reword the Assessment description to name established small and growing businesses as the primary audience. Reword the Codex description to the exact recommended positioning. Add a short "Your Path" block to the Resources page stating both journeys.

**Proposed replacement copy:** See complete copy section below.

**Reason:** Matches the required product positioning for both the Assessment and the Codex, and makes the client journey and the institutional pathway visible on the one page built to hold both products.

---

## 7. Why Organizations Choose Calden Terre (Home, renamed from "Why Calden Terre")

**Current positioning problem:** Three cards (Implementation-focused, Systems thinking, Long-term value) do not match the four required themes.

**Recommended change:** Rename the section and expand to four cards matching the required themes exactly: Implementation-Focused, Data and Decision Support, Practical Systems, Accountability and Governance. The existing four-column grid class already used elsewhere on the site accommodates this without any new CSS.

**Proposed replacement copy:** See complete copy section below.

**Reason:** This is an explicit, named requirement in the brief, and the four-column grid pattern already exists on the site (used for the framework's responsive layout), so no new visual system is introduced.

---

## 8. Services page

**Current positioning problem:** Card descriptions carry ESG alphabet-soup language ("materiality," "ESG readiness," "framework alignment") and present six areas as separate rather than connected, with no responsible sourcing capability represented at all.

**Recommended change:** Reword the intro to state the capabilities work as one system. Re-map the six cards to the ten required capability areas, in plainer language, adding responsible sourcing and supply chain support where climate impact currently stands alone.

**Reason:** Matches the tone requirement to be free of unnecessary jargon and organizes capabilities around the implementation journey rather than presenting a broad, disconnected list.

---

## 9. About page

**Current positioning problem:** Opens with generic "Organizations increasingly recognize..." language that could describe any consultancy, and states the audience as "SMEs and Community Development Financial Institutions."

**Recommended change:** Open with the exact required positioning statement. Rewrite "Our purpose" to name the specific gaps established small and growing businesses face (priorities, systems, accountability, measurement, execution, communication). Add a line in "How we work" naming the institutional partnership model explicitly, so About carries the full audience structure in prose form.

**Reason:** About is the one page where both the direct-client story and the institutional-partner story can sit together in full sentences, giving the rest of the site room to stay shorter.

---

## 10. Contact page ("How Can We Support You?")

**Current positioning problem:** The form only captures name, email, organization, and a free-text message. It has no way to route a CDFI or business-support inquiry differently from a direct business inquiry, and no structured way to see what kind of support someone wants before they write it out.

**Recommended change:** Add an "Organization Type" select and an "Areas of Support" checkbox group, using the exact options and categories specified. Both use the existing field styling (the same border, radius, and focus treatment already applied to the name and email inputs) so the form's visual language does not change.

**Reason:** Directly supports the required organization-type options and areas-of-support list, and gives Calden Terre a structured signal on whether an inquiry is a direct client or a potential institutional partner, without changing the form's premium, minimal appearance.

---

## 11. Footer

**Current positioning problem:** Tagline repeats the "SMEs and Community Development Financial Institutions" equal-audience phrasing.

**Recommended change:** Reword to the new positioning, in one sentence naming the primary audience and a second short sentence naming the institutional partnership.

**Reason:** The footer appears on every page, so it is the most repeated audience statement on the site and needs to match the new structure precisely.

---

## 12. Resource capture component (email opt-in copy, used on Home and Resources)

**Current positioning problem:** "for SMEs and CDFIs" appears in the opt-in paragraph.

**Recommended change:** Reword to "for established small and growing businesses."

**Reason:** Small, repeated phrase; same fix as the hero and footer.

---

## 13. Site metadata (page titles and descriptions, not visible on page but used by search engines and link previews)

**Current positioning problem:** Root layout description and About/Services/Resources metadata all repeat "SMEs and Community Development Financial Institutions" or generic "organizations" language.

**Recommended change:** Update each page's metadata description to match its on-page copy.

**Reason:** Keeps what search engines and shared links show consistent with the new positioning, since this text is otherwise invisible and easy to miss.

---

## Sections remaining substantially unchanged

- Site navigation and header, footer link structure, brand mark and name.
- The "Our Thesis" statement band on the homepage (already states the implementation gap thesis precisely, no SME or CDFI language).
- The Selected Work page and its four case studies, in full.
- The Insights page (no audience-specific language present).
- All visual design: palette, typography, spacing, animation, card and icon system, motion on scroll and page load.
